Project purpose

Making mathematics more approachable

The project is aimed at high-school, college and introductory university learners, as well as teachers and independent learners who want an interactive support tool.

The full challenge roadmap will focus on accuracy, reducing misleading answers, improving prompt consistency, supporting different learning levels, and maintaining a simple user experience.
